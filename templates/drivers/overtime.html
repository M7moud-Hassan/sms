On an off day every hour counts, from the first begin to the last end. The before/after rules above do not apply.
Overtime shorter than this (per day: before work, after work, or off day) is ignored.
How it is calculated: for each driver and day, work runs from the first voucher begin (receipt from) to the last voucher end (receipt to). On a work day only the part before the begin time or after the end time is overtime; on an off day the whole span is overtime. A night trip that ends after midnight stays on the day it began.
Examples: work day 08:00–16:00, last delivery 18:30 → 2.50 h × 1.25 = 3.13 weighted hours. Off day, first begin 10:00, last end 14:30 → 4.50 h × 1.50 = 6.75 weighted hours.
